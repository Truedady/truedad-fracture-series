# TrueDad / Premium Market Motion — Design system

Core visual direction: premium financial terminal + high-end sports broadcast typography, adapted to CS2. Not casino, betting, generic gaming neon, or a crypto pump channel.

## Color tokens
- Background Graphite: `#0B0E13`
- Panels: `#151A22`
- Primary text Warm Ivory: `#F4F1E9`
- Brand Accent Muted Gold: `#D9A93D`
- Lines: `#2B3440`
- Secondary text: `#98A3B5`
- Down only: `#E54C58`
- Up only: `#26A685`

Restrict gold to a 5–15% share of on-screen area. Never use yellow for random emphasis; use red/green exclusively for real market directions.

## Typography
Heavy clean grotesk for key numbers and short lines (Arial Black / system sans fallback). Monospace for tables, prices, counters. Avoid too many families.

## Motion
Responsive 8–14 frame transitions, minimal springs, elegant masked reveals, numerical counters, real crop/pan into actual screenshots, synchronized to narration. Never overwhelm reading time. No decorative lens flares, fake glitches, spin transitions.

## Vertical safe-area
1080×1920. Keep crucial info at least 100px from left/right, 250px from bottom, 150px from top when plausible. Never cover essential figures with platform UI.

## Structural beats
0–3s: falling market vs our increasing purchases (strong contrast)
3–12s: premise + case
12–23s: precise 20 / 25 / 30 ...165 progression, 2775 total
23–36s: actual Steam purchase proof, inventory evidence
36–44s: completed day 1, tomorrow 25, brief comment CTA

## Factual ethics
No fabricated candles, trades, performance figures, or supply scarcity. Always distinguish listed asks from executed purchases. Include fees in realized net P&L; keep unavailable fields null.
